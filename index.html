Indicator Definitions
Voter Turnout

The percentage of registered voters in a community district who cast ballots in the most recent municipal general election cycle. This figure does not reflect the share of the total adult population — only those already on the voter rolls. Low turnout may signal civic disengagement, language barriers, or poll accessibility issues.

Public Health Mandate

The share of votes cast for candidates who made lead pipe remediation, clean water access, and environmental health investment central planks of their platform. This layer captures whether communities most affected by contamination are translating health concerns into electoral support for policy change.

Lead in Water

Estimated average lead concentration in residential tap water, measured in parts per billion (ppb). The U.S. EPA's action level is 15 ppb — above which utilities must take corrective action. Exposure is linked to older lead service lines and pre-1986 plumbing fixtures, which are concentrated in lower-income neighborhoods.

Educational Outcomes

A composite index (0–100) combining standardized proficiency rates in grades 3–8 and 4-year high school graduation rates for public schools within the district, normalized against the citywide mean. Peer-reviewed research establishes that chronic lead exposure at even low levels measurably depresses this metric.

Methodology & Data Sources
Illustrative Data — Not for Policy Use

All figures displayed in this tool are simulated estimates created for educational and research communication purposes. They are calibrated to reflect patterns documented in peer-reviewed public-health literature, but they are not derived from official measurements. They should not be cited or used for policy, legal, or advocacy purposes.

Indicator Construction

  • Voter Turnout — Percentage of registered voters casting ballots in the most recent NYC general election cycle, by community district. Reflects only those already enrolled; does not account for disenfranchisement or registration gaps.
  • Public Health Mandate — Share of votes received by candidates who formally committed to lead pipe replacement, increased water testing, and environmental health budget allocations. Candidacy records were coded by platform stance.
  • Lead in Water — Average residential tap water lead concentration in parts per billion (ppb), estimated from service-line material records and building age data. The EPA Lead and Copper Rule action level is 15 ppb.
  • Educational Outcomes — Composite index (0–100) weighting NYS Grades 3–8 ELA and Math proficiency rates (60%) and 4-year graduation rates (40%), normalized to a citywide baseline of 65.

Evidence Base

The design of this tool is informed by a substantial body of epidemiological research. Key findings include:

  • Blood lead levels above 5 µg/dL are associated with a 1–5 point reduction in IQ in children (Lanphear et al., 2005)
  • Neighborhood-level lead exposure explains a statistically significant portion of school test score variance after controlling for income (Sampson & Winter, 2018)
  • Communities with elevated environmental burdens exhibit measurably lower voter registration and turnout rates (Hajnal et al., 2017)
